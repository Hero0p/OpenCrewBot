## Market research: Indian bakery subscription boxes and Pune consumer preferences
**Question:** What are the urban Indian food retail trends, subscription box dynamics, and Pune consumer preferences relevant to launching a monthly bakery subscription box for a 4-store chain?  
**Scope:** Urban India and Pune, 2025–2026.

### Key findings
- **India Subscription Box Market Growth:** The Indian subscription box market was valued at USD 1,379.4 million in 2025 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 12.04% from 2026 to 2034, reaching USD 3,954.6 million ([IMARC Group 2026](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-subscription-box-market)).
- **Broad India Bakery Market Context:** The broader Indian bakery market is valued at approximately ₹1.7 to ₹2 lakh crore ($15.06B to $16.4B in 2026) with an 8–10% CAGR ([IMARC Group / Commodity Champions 2026](https://www.imarcgroup.com)).
- **Urban Consumer Shift:** Urban Indian consumers increasingly demand convenience, discovery of new flavors (such as fusion bakery and gourmet cookies), and consistent freshness rather than traditional one-off purchasing ([Indusfood 2026](https://www.facebook.com/indusfoodshow/posts/changing-consumer-demands-in-industry)).
- **Subscription Fatigue & Churn Factors:** Industry data indicates that food subscription churn can be high due to recurring bill fatigue and cost concerns (approx. 46% of consumers cite unwillingness to take on recurring bills as a primary barrier) ([CNET / Plastic Container City 2025](https://plasticcontainercity.com/blog/post/how-to-start-a-bakery-subscription-box-that-actually-makes-money)). Local bakery boxes mitigate this better than national shipped boxes because local delivery guarantees freshness and leverages established community brand trust.

### Market size and trends
