### Summary of Findings

1. **IDP Education (India)**
   - **Positioning:** Traditional and omnichannel market leader with an extensive physical branch network and co-ownership of IELTS.
   - **Key Product Lines:** University placements, visa assistance, pre-departure support, and IELTS testing.
   - **Pricing:** Free for students (monetized via B2B university commissions).

2. **Leap Scholar**
   - **Positioning:** Tech-first edtech ecosystem backed by Owl Ventures, Sequoia/Jungle Ventures ($150M+ total funding, near $850M–$1B valuation).
   - **Key Product Lines:** Study abroad counselling, test prep (IELTS/TOEFL/GRE), international student loans (Leap Finance), and visa support.
   - **Pricing:** Freemium model (free basic counseling; paid test prep packages and financial products).

3. **Leverage Edu**
   - **Positioning:** Digital-first platform utilizing proprietary AI tools and virtual fairs, targeting ₹600 crore revenue in FY27 ahead of a proposed public listing.
   - **Key Product Lines:** AI-driven university matching (Course Finder), application & SOP/LOR guidance, test prep (Leverage Live), accommodation, and student loans.
   - **Pricing:** Free student counselling supported by university commissions, alongside paid coaching and financial services.
