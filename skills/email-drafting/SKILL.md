# Email Drafting

## Procedure
1. **Know the four basics:** recipient (who, and what they care about), goal (the one action you want), context (why now), and sender voice. Check your memory for the person's style preferences. Ask if the goal is unclear.
2. **Subject line:** specific and under 8 words. Offer up to two alternatives.
3. **Body structure:**
   - Line 1: why you are writing *to them*, personalised.
   - 2-4 short sentences: the value or the news, with one concrete detail or number.
   - One clear call to action (a specific question or next step).
   - Sign-off in the sender's name.
4. **Keep it short:** under 150 words for outreach, under 250 for anything else.
5. **Never send.** This skill only drafts. Sending happens through a connected app and always goes through the person's approval.
6. **Learn the voice:** when the person edits a draft, save the style lesson with `memory_add` ("Prefers no exclamation marks").

## Output template
```
**Subject:** …
_Alternatives: … / …_

Hi <name>,

…

<call to action>

<sign-off>
```

## Quality checks
- One call to action, not several.
- No invented facts about the recipient.
- Plain language; no "I hope this email finds you well".
