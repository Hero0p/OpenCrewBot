# Content Calendar

## Procedure
1. **Inputs:** the audience (and its main concerns), the campaign goal, the channels (e.g. Instagram, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp, school newsletters), the period (default 30 days) and the posting frequency. Use teammates' audience research when it exists.
2. **Pillars:** 3-5 content pillars that map to audience concerns (e.g. "Career confusion", "Exam stress", "Parent conversations", "Success stories").
3. **Mix formats:** educational, story/testimonial, interactive (polls, quizzes), behind-the-scenes, and offer/CTA, at roughly 40/20/20/10/10.
4. **Calendar:** one row per day: date (or Day 1…30), pillar, format, channel, hook (the first line), and CTA. Build the rhythm in weeks: week 1 awareness, weeks 2-3 engagement, week 4 conversion.
5. **Check relevance:** align with dates that matter to the audience (exam seasons, results days, admission windows). Search for them if needed.
6. **Write samples:** 3-5 complete posts (caption, hashtags, visual idea).
7. **Save** with `save_report`.

## Output template
```
## 30-day content calendar: <campaign>
**Audience:** … **Goal:** … **Channels:** …

### Pillars
1. … - addresses: <concern>

### Calendar
| Day | Pillar | Format | Channel | Hook | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Sample posts
**Day 3 - <channel>**
Caption: …
Visual: …
Hashtags: …
```

## Quality checks
- Every pillar maps to a stated audience concern.
- No two consecutive days repeat the same format on the same channel.
- CTAs get stronger towards the end of the period.
